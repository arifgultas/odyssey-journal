2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Account Credentials: Email address, hashed & salted password, username, full name, profile bio, avatar image.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Travel posts, stories, photographs, comments, bookmarks, collections, reactions (likes), travel dates, and optional destination locations.
  • Direct Communications: In-app direct messages and correspondence sent to our support channels.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device & OS Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, preferred language, and time zone.
  • Diagnostic & Crash Logs: Crash logs and diagnostic metrics captured through Sentry (strictly scrubbed of Personally Identifiable Information - PII).
  • Network & App Telemetry: Anonymized IP address, connectivity status, and app launch timestamps.

C. Location Information

Precise Location (GPS): Location coordinates are captured only when you explicitly choose to tag a travel post with your current location. We never collect continuous or background location data. Location permissions may be revoked at any time in device settings.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Supabase Architecture

Odyssey Journal utilizes Supabase (https://supabase.com) as its core cloud backend, database, authentication provider, and object storage engine.

  • Server Region: Our primary production database and media storage are hosted on Supabase enterprise infrastructure located in AWS EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmissions between the App and Supabase are encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database records and uploaded media are stored with AES-256 hardware-level encryption.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Granular PostgreSQL security policies isolate data access cryptographically, guaranteeing that only authenticated users can access their permitted datasets.
  • Token Security: JWT session tokens are stored securely on the device using native iOS Keychain and Android Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences.
  • Automated Backups: Supabase Pro tier daily backups and point-in-time recovery ensure business continuity.

8. Rights Under Turkish Law (KVKK Madde 11)

Türkiye Cumhuriyeti 6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu ("KVKK") m. 11 uyarınca, veri sahibi olarak aşağıdaki haklara sahipsiniz:

  • Kişisel verilerinizin işlenip işlenmediğini öğrenme ve bilgi talep etme,
  • İşlenme amacını ve amacına uygun kullanılıp kullanılmadığını öğrenme,
  • Yurt içinde veya yurt dışında verilerin aktarıldığı üçüncü kişileri bilme,
  • Eksik veya yanlış işlenen verilerin düzeltilmesini isteme,
  • KVKK m. 7 uyarınca verilerinizin silinmesini veya yok edilmesini talep etme,
  • Otomatik sistemlerle analiz neticesinde aleyhinize bir sonuca itiraz etme,
  • Kanuna aykırı işleme nedeniyle zarara uğramanız hâlinde zararın giderilmesini talep etme.

KVKK kapsamındaki başvurularınızı kimliğinizi teyit eden bilgilerle privacy@odysseyjournal.app adresine iletebilirsiniz. Başvurular en geç 30 gün içinde sonuçlandırılır.
