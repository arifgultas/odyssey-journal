## Full Description

**Your travel story, beautifully told.**

Odyssey Journal is the ultimate travel companion for documenting and sharing your adventures. Capture moments with photos, tag locations on an interactive map, and connect with a global community of fellow travelers.

**📸 Capture & Share**
• Create rich travel posts with multiple photos and captions
• Tag locations and see weather conditions automatically
• Organize posts into custom collections
• Share your adventures with the community

**🗺️ Explore the World**
• Browse an interactive map of destinations
• Discover popular posts from around the globe
• Explore posts by destination, category, or popularity
• See what's trending in the travel community

**👥 Connect & Engage**
• Follow your favorite travelers
• Like, comment, and bookmark posts
• Get real-time notifications for interactions
• View shared destinations with other users

**📔 Personal Journal**
• Beautiful passport-themed profile page
• Track countries visited and kilometers traveled
• Dark mode for comfortable reading
• Available in 12 languages

**🔒 Privacy & Security**
• Your data is encrypted and securely stored
• Full account deletion anytime
• No ads, no tracking, no data selling
